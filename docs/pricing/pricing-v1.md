# Pricing v1 — RSP AI Editor

## Plan Matrix

### Free ($0)
- 5 image edits/day
- 10 copy rewrites/day
- Standard export quality
- Local session history only
- Watermark on exports [if enabled]
- CTA: Start Free

### Pro ($12/mo or $108/year)
- 1,200 credits/month
- Effective annual price: $9/mo (75% of monthly list when billed yearly)
- HD exports
- No watermark
- Priority processing
- Batch export (up to 20 per batch)
- CTA: Choose Pro

### Max ($28/mo or $252/year)
- 3,500 credits/month
- Effective annual price: $21/mo (75% of monthly list when billed yearly)
- HD exports
- No watermark
- Priority processing
- Batch export (up to 20 per batch)
- CTA: Upgrade to Max

### Credit Packs
- Starter Pack: 500 credits — $6.9 (first purchase only)
- Standard Pack: 1,500 credits — $26.9
- Growth Pack: 3,000 credits — $48.9
- Scale Pack: 6,000 credits — $86.9

### Credit Rules
- Paid plans use monthly credit allocations instead of separate edit-count buckets.
- Credits reset each billing cycle and do not roll over.
- Starter Pack is a one-time acquisition offer and must be labeled as first-purchase-only.
- Credit packs are overflow purchases for Pro/Max users or one-off paid users; they should not be positioned as cheaper than subscribing.

## Guardrails
- No “unlimited” claims
- Free tier should show value but prevent abuse
- Launch pricing should be presented as Free / Pro / Max + Credit Packs only
- Do not market Team or Lifetime in v1 until product, billing, and entitlement support exist
- Reprice if gross margin < 65%
